About the Enigma Machine

The Enigma machine was a cipher device developed and used in the early- to mid-20th century to protect commercial, diplomatic, and military communication.

How to Use
  1. Configure the rotors by selecting rotor types and initial positions
  2. Set up plugboard connections by clicking pairs of letters
  3. Type your message using the keyboard or your physical keyboard
  4. Watch the encryption process and signal path visualization
Historical Significance

The breaking of Enigma-encrypted messages by the Polish Cipher Bureau, and later by the British at Bletchley Park (where Alan Turing worked), played an important role in Allied victory in WWII.
