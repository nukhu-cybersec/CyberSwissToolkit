  • Enable two-factor authentication when available

About the Enigma Machine

The Enigma machine was used by Nazi Germany during World War II to encrypt sensitive military communications. It used a system of rotors that would change with each keystroke, creating an encryption that was extremely difficult to break at the time.

British codebreakers at Bletchley Park, including Alan Turing, developed techniques and machines to decrypt Enigma messages, which significantly contributed to the Allied victory.
